# Field AI

> FieldAI builds general-purpose robot intelligence through its Field Foundation Models (FFMs) and EDGE brain — physics-first, risk-aware foundation models for embodied AI. FieldAI's embodiment-agnostic autonomy lets robots navigate dynamic, unstructured environments without maps, GPS, or predefined trajectories. Founded in 2023 (formerly AI for Humanity) and based in Mission Viejo, California, the company serves construction, energy, mining, logistics, manufacturing, power and utility, agriculture, and federal applications.
